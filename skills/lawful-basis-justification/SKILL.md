# Lawful Basis Justification

Every processing purpose needs a documented GDPR Article 6 basis chosen before processing starts. Review tests the fit: legitimate interests need a recorded balancing test, contract necessity must be genuinely necessary, and legal obligations must be cited to the specific law. Special-category data additionally requires an Article 9(2) condition.

## Watch for
- Processing with no documented Article 6 basis, or a basis selected after the fact.
- Consent chosen where another basis fits, then becoming unmanageable (withdrawals, granularity demands).
- Legitimate interests claimed without the three-part test: legitimate aim, necessity, balancing against data-subject rights.
- Contract necessity (Article 6(1)(b)) claimed for processing not objectively necessary to perform the contract (analytics, marketing).
- Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) cited without identifying the specific law.
- Special-category data processed without an Article 9(2) condition alongside Article 6.
- Basis drift: data collected under consent but later used under legitimate interests without re-basing.
- Public-task or vital-interest bases invoked by private controllers without justification.

## Best practices
- Select and document one primary Article 6 basis per purpose before processing starts.
- For legitimate interests, run and record an LIA: purpose legitimacy, necessity, and balancing test with mitigations.
- Use contract necessity only for processing genuinely required to perform the contract with that data subject.
- Cite the specific legal obligation underlying Article 6(1)(c).
- Pair every special-category processing with a documented Article 9(2) condition.
- Where consent is used, hold it to the GDPR standard: freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, withdrawable, recorded.
- Re-assess the basis whenever the purpose changes; never silently re-purpose.
- Reflect each basis in the privacy notice (Articles 13/14) and the RoPA.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Article 6 basis documented per purpose.
- [ ] LIA completed for legitimate interests.
- [ ] Contract necessity genuinely necessary.
- [ ] Legal obligation specifically cited.
- [ ] Article 9 condition for special categories.
- [ ] Consent meets GDPR standard if used.
- [ ] Basis reflected in notice and RoPA.
