# Legal Disclaimer Insertion

Contract review output informs; it does not advise. Disclaimers must be prominent, match the actual scope of review, and accompany every enforceability statement. The failure mode is confident prescriptive language with a buried, generic disclaimer — the exact inverse of what a reviewer should produce.

## Watch for
- Contract analysis presented as legal advice without "not legal advice — consult counsel" framing.
- Enforceability conclusions stated as fact rather than flagged for attorney review.
- Jurisdiction-specific conclusions without noting they depend on local law.
- Missing "attorney review required" flags on high-stakes clauses (IP assignment, uncapped liability, regulatory exposure).
- Disclaimers buried at the end after confident, directive language.
- Template disclaimers that misstate the scope of review actually performed.
- Privilege or confidentiality assumptions stated without basis.
- Recommendations phrased as instructions to sign or accept without counsel.

## Best practices
- Frame all output as review flags and observations for human counsel, never as legal advice or opinions.
- State the review scope: what was checked and what was not (e.g., "clause text reviewed; no verification of facts or external law").
- Flag jurisdiction-dependent issues explicitly and recommend local counsel.
- Place disclaimers prominently — at the top of summaries and beside high-risk findings — not only at the end.
- Use conditional language for enforceability: "may be unenforceable in X," "courts in Y have treated..." — never categorical.
- Match disclaimer scope to actual work performed; disclaim neither more nor less.
- Phrase next steps as "consider discussing with counsel," not directives.
- Note that clause review does not substitute for negotiation strategy advice.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] "Not legal advice" statement present and prominent.
- [ ] Review scope stated explicitly.
- [ ] Jurisdiction-dependent items flagged.
- [ ] Enforceability language conditional.
- [ ] High-stakes items marked for attorney review.
- [ ] Disclaimer matches actual review scope.
- [ ] Next steps framed as counsel discussion.
