# Non-Compete Enforceability

Restrictive covenants — non-competes, non-solicits, non-deal — are among the most jurisdiction-dependent provisions in any contract. Some jurisdictions void employee non-competes almost entirely; others enforce only what is reasonably necessary to protect a legitimate interest. Review flags overbreadth and forum risk; it never concludes enforceability as fact.

## Watch for
- Non-competes in jurisdictions where they are banned or heavily restricted for employees (e.g., California, Oklahoma, and North Dakota broadly void them; others impose salary thresholds or notice duties — check current local law).
- Duration, geography, or activity scope broader than needed to protect the stated interest.
- No consideration for the restriction — required in many jurisdictions, especially for mid-term additions.
- Non-solicitation and non-deal clauses carrying the same overbreadth defects.
- No garden-leave or compensation-during-restriction provision where local law requires it.
- Blue-pencil assumptions: some jurisdictions reform overbroad clauses, others void them entirely.
- Employee-style restrictions applied to independent contractors (compounds misclassification risk).
- Missing carve-outs for publicly available information or residual knowledge/skills.

## Best practices
- Check the governing jurisdiction first: statutory bans, thresholds, and notice requirements vary radically and change frequently.
- Require the restriction to name a legitimate business interest (trade secrets, confidential information, customer relationships).
- Keep duration short (months, not years, for employees), geography tied to actual business footprint, scope tied to actual competitive activity.
- Verify consideration: signed at hire, or fresh consideration (promotion, payment) for later additions.
- Assess non-compete, customer non-solicit, employee non-solicit, and confidentiality separately — enforceability differs.
- Note the forum's blue-pencil rule; overbreadth can void the entire clause where reformation is unavailable.
- Flag contractor non-competes as misclassification risk.
- Recommend local-counsel review — enforceability is fact- and forum-specific; flag, don't conclude.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Governing jurisdiction identified and checked for bans.
- [ ] Legitimate interest stated in the clause.
- [ ] Duration/geography/scope tailored and reasonable.
- [ ] Consideration documented.
- [ ] Non-solicit/non-deal assessed separately.
- [ ] Forum's blue-pencil rule noted.
- [ ] Contractor vs employee status verified.
