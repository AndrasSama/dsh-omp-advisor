# AP Style Compliance

Consistent style is a credibility signal: readers and peers notice drift, and mixed conventions make a desk look careless. This skill reviews copy against core AP Stylebook conventions. The standing rule: the current AP Stylebook and documented house overrides win any conflict.

## Watch for
- Numbers handled against the rule (spell one through nine; figures for 10 and above, with standard exceptions).
- Ages, percentages, dimensions, and sums of money spelled out instead of using figures.
- Titles capitalized after a name or lowercased before one, inconsistently.
- State names spelled out where AP abbreviates, or postal codes used where AP uses its own abbreviations.
- Dates with ordinal suffixes ("May 3rd") or unnecessary "on."
- Overcapitalization of job descriptions, departments, and generic program names.
- Times with colons at zero minutes, or "a.m./p.m." styled incorrectly.
- Inconsistent capitalization of composition titles, government bodies, and seasons.

## Best practices
- Apply the numerals rule systematically: spell one–nine, figures for 10+, with AP exceptions (ages, money, percentages, dimensions take figures).
- Capitalize formal titles only immediately before a name; lowercase them standing alone or after the name.
- Use AP state abbreviations per the Stylebook list; follow current AP guidance on spelling out state names.
- Write dates without ordinals; place commas correctly around the year in full dates.
- Lowercase seasons, generic program names, and job descriptions not preceding a name.
- Style times as "3 p.m.," not "3:00 p.m." or "3 PM."
- Keep a house style sheet recording legitimate overrides to AP.
- Run a dedicated style pass separate from the fact-check pass.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Numerals follow the one-through-nine rule and its exceptions.
- [ ] Ages, money, percentages, and dimensions use figures.
- [ ] Titles are capitalized only directly before names.
- [ ] State names/abbreviations match AP usage.
- [ ] Dates are free of ordinals and unnecessary "on."
- [ ] Capitalization of departments and programs was checked.
- [ ] Times are styled per AP (no :00, lowercase a.m./p.m.).
- [ ] House overrides are documented and applied consistently.
