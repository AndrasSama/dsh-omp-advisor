# Terms of Service & EULA Audit

Standard-form consumer contracts are reviewable for significant imbalance between the parties' rights and obligations. The annex of EU Directive 93/13/EEC supplies an indicative list of terms that may be regarded as unfair, and this skill uses it as a practical red-flag checklist. Findings are risk indicators for legal review, never binding legal advice.

## Watch for
- Unilateral change rights letting the provider alter terms, prices, or the service without a valid reason specified in the contract.
- Terms binding consumers to hidden or post-signature conditions they had no real chance to read.
- Lopsided liability: provider excludes all liability while the consumer carries unlimited obligations.
- Termination or suspension at sole provider discretion, without notice or refund of prepaid amounts.
- Mandatory arbitration or forum-selection clauses imposed on consumers without genuine choice.
- Auto-renewal buried in dense prose with no clear cancellation path.
- Disproportionate liquidated damages or forfeiture when the consumer cancels.
- Unreadable presentation: wall-of-caps text, extreme length, no headings or summary.

## Best practices
- Walk the 93/13/EEC annex categories one by one and record each matching clause with a verbatim quote.
- Assess prominence, not just content: a fair term hidden in clause 37.2 can still be a surprising term.
- Check plain-language readability; flag sentences a typical consumer cannot parse on first reading.
- Verify change clauses include notice, a valid reason, and a genuine right to exit.
- Compare termination rights on both sides for symmetry and prepaid-balance treatment.
- Confirm the contract does not make consumers waive statutory rights (guarantees, withdrawal).
- Recommend a plain-language summary layer for long documents.
- Escalate suspected unfair terms to counsel, ranking severity by how central the term is to the contract.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] No unilateral change rights without notice + exit option.
- [ ] Liability allocation not one-sided.
- [ ] Termination/suspension rights roughly symmetric, prepaid amounts refunded.
- [ ] No forced arbitration or distant forum selection for consumers.
- [ ] Auto-renewal prominent with a clear cancellation path.
- [ ] No waiver of statutory guarantees or withdrawal rights.
- [ ] Document readable: headings, summary, plain language.
- [ ] Every flagged clause quoted verbatim and escalated to counsel.
