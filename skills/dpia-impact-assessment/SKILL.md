# DPIA Impact Assessment

A Data Protection Impact Assessment (GDPR Article 35) is required before high-risk processing begins and must drive real mitigation decisions. Review checks both the screening (was a DPIA required and done in time?) and the substance (are risks scored, mitigations mapped, residual risk judged?). A DPIA performed after launch, or one with unlinked mitigations, is a compliance defect.

## Watch for
- DPIA missing where Article 35(3) requires one: systematic extensive profiling with legal/significant effects, large-scale special-category processing, or systematic monitoring of publicly accessible areas.
- DPIA conducted after processing started instead of prior.
- Risk assessment without likelihood × severity scoring, or without naming risks to data subjects' rights.
- Mitigations listed but not mapped to the specific risks they address.
- DPO not consulted where Article 35(2) requires it.
- Supervisory-authority consultation (Article 36) skipped despite high residual risk.
- DPIA not revisited after material changes to the processing.
- Generic DPIA that never describes the actual processing, necessity, or proportionality.

## Best practices
- Screen every new processing activity against Article 35(3) triggers and the relevant supervisory authority's published lists.
- Conduct the DPIA before processing begins; document the decision even when one is not required.
- Structure: processing description and purposes → necessity/proportionality → risks to data subjects → mitigations → residual risk.
- Score risks on likelihood × severity and name concrete harms (discrimination, identity theft, loss of confidentiality).
- Map each mitigation to a risk with an owner and a date.
- Consult the DPO per Article 35(2) and record the consultation.
- If residual risk remains high, consult the supervisory authority under Article 36 before proceeding.
- Define review triggers: processing changes, new data types, incidents, or elapsed time.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Article 35 screening documented.
- [ ] DPIA performed prior to processing.
- [ ] Risks scored on likelihood × severity.
- [ ] Mitigations mapped to risks with owners.
- [ ] DPO consultation recorded.
- [ ] Article 36 path assessed for high residual risk.
- [ ] Review triggers defined.
