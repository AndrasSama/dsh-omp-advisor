# Termination Rights Analysis

Termination analysis inventories every exit path in an agreement and what happens when it is pulled. Ambiguity here is costly: undefined cure periods, missing effects-of-termination terms, and incomplete survival lists generate disputes exactly when the relationship is already failing. Compute real dates from auto-renewals rather than assuming they exist.

## Watch for
- Termination for convenience missing, or one-sided without notice period and payment consequences.
- Cure periods absent, too short, or defined inconsistently across breach types.
- "Material breach" undefined, leaving termination triggers ambiguous.
- Effects of termination missing: data return/destruction, license fate, work in progress, prepayments.
- Survival clause that fails to list surviving sections (confidentiality, LoL, indemnity, dispute resolution).
- Ipso facto (insolvency) termination clauses that may be unenforceable — e.g., restricted under US bankruptcy law; jurisdiction-dependent.
- Auto-renewal with a termination-notice window that has already passed or is impractically short.
- No wind-down or transition-assistance terms for critical services.

## Best practices
- Enumerate every termination right: for cause, for convenience, for insolvency, change of control, and notice-based.
- For each right, record trigger, notice requirement, cure period, and effective-date mechanics.
- Verify effects of termination: data handling, license revocation or survival, payment for services rendered, refund of prepayments.
- Check the survival list explicitly includes confidentiality, LoL, indemnity, and dispute resolution.
- Flag ipso facto clauses and note enforceability is jurisdiction-dependent.
- For auto-renewals, compute the actual termination-notice deadline from the effective date and renewal term.
- Require transition-assistance terms (scope, duration, fees) for critical services.
- Confirm termination preserves accrued rights: payments due and breaches already committed.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] All termination rights enumerated.
- [ ] Notice and cure periods defined per right.
- [ ] Effects of termination specified.
- [ ] Survival list complete.
- [ ] Auto-renewal notice deadline computed.
- [ ] Transition assistance covered.
- [ ] Accrued rights preserved.
