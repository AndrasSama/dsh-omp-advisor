# Vendor DPA Review

A data processing agreement operationalizes GDPR Article 28 between controller and processor. Review checks the mandatory content of Article 28(3)(a)–(h) and the provisions that actually determine compliance in practice: sub-processor control, breach and SAR assistance, security annexes, end-of-service deletion, and usable audit rights.

## Watch for
- Missing Article 28(3) mandatory terms: subject matter, duration, nature and purpose, data categories, data-subject categories, controller obligations.
- No "process only on documented instructions" clause, or one weakened by broad discretion.
- No confidentiality commitment for processor personnel.
- Security measures (Article 32) referenced but unspecified — no annex or concrete controls.
- Sub-processor regime gaps: no prior authorization, no flow-down of obligations, no change notification with objection rights.
- Assistance obligations missing for SARs, DPIAs, and breach notification.
- End-of-service terms absent: deletion or return of data (Article 28(3)(g)) without clarity on lawful retention.
- Audit rights absent or practically unusable (excessive notice, prohibitive cost, no third-party-report substitution).

## Best practices
- Check all Article 28(3)(a)–(h) elements are present and specific to the engagement.
- Require documented-instruction processing with a defined instruction mechanism.
- Verify sub-processor controls: approved-list annex, notification period, objection/termination rights, full flow-down of obligations.
- Confirm assistance duties: SAR support within timelines, DPIA input, breach cooperation with prompt notification.
- Specify security measures in an annex (encryption, access control, testing cadence) with change-notification duties.
- Define end-of-service: deletion or return, format, timeline, certification; isolate any legally required retention.
- Make audit rights practical: annual audits, reasonable notice, SOC 2/ISO 27001 reports as a supplement.
- Align the DPA with the correct SCC module where international transfers occur.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Article 28(3) elements complete.
- [ ] Documented-instructions clause present.
- [ ] Sub-processor controls and flow-down verified.
- [ ] Assistance duties (SAR/DPIA/breach) present.
- [ ] Security measures annex attached.
- [ ] Deletion/return terms specific.
- [ ] Audit rights practical.
