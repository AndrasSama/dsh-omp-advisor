# Withdrawal Right Workflow Review

Distance and off-premises contracts in the EU carry a 14-day withdrawal right under Directive 2011/83/EU, and the workflow around it matters as much as the policy text. This skill reviews whether the right is informed correctly, exercisable easily, and honored properly, including the limited exceptions. Findings are review flags for legal review, not legal advice.

## Watch for
- Missing or wrong information about the 14-day period and how to exercise withdrawal.
- No model withdrawal form or equivalent clear statement mechanism offered.
- Withdrawal harder than ordering: phone-only cancellation, mandatory account, hidden forms.
- Misapplied exceptions (digital content, bespoke/personalized goods, sealed hygiene items) without meeting their specific conditions, e.g. express consent plus acknowledgment of losing the right for digital content.
- Refunds delayed beyond the required window (generally 14 days from withdrawal notice) or gated behind unreasonable hurdles.
- Refunds forced as store credit only instead of the original means of payment.
- Return-shipping cost allocation not disclosed before the order.
- Withdrawal period computed from the wrong start point (order date instead of possession of goods).

## Best practices
- Verify withdrawal information is provided before the order and again in the order confirmation.
- Test the actual withdrawal path end-to-end: locate the form, submit, receive acknowledgment.
- For digital content, confirm the express-consent plus loss-of-right acknowledgment flow is implemented, not just claimed.
- Check refund timing and that the default is reimbursement via the original payment means.
- Confirm who bears return shipping costs and that this was disclosed pre-contract.
- Ensure support scripts do not pressure consumers away from exercising withdrawal.
- Validate period calculation starts from possession (goods) or contract conclusion (services/digital).
- Escalate ambiguous exception claims (bespoke, perishable, sealed) to counsel with exact product facts.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] 14-day right and exercise method disclosed pre-contract.
- [ ] Model withdrawal form or clear statement mechanism available.
- [ ] Withdrawal path no harder than the purchase path.
- [ ] Exceptions applied only where their conditions are genuinely met.
- [ ] Digital-content consent + acknowledgment flow verified.
- [ ] Refund within the required window via original payment means.
- [ ] Return shipping cost disclosed before order.
- [ ] Period start date computed correctly.
