# Subscription Cancellation Ease

Regulators increasingly expect cancellation to be as easy as enrollment — FTC negative-option enforcement and click-to-cancel principles make same-medium cancellation the baseline. This skill reviews subscription flows for friction engineered to trap users. Findings are review flags, not legal advice.

## Watch for
- Sign-up available online but cancellation only by phone, mail, or live chat during limited hours.
- Cancellation buried behind multiple screens, account walls, or mandatory retention-agent conversations.
- Required wait times, cooling-off periods, or "call to confirm" steps absent from sign-up.
- Retention flows that misrepresent what cancellation does (e.g., falsely claiming access ends immediately).
- Guilt-laden or misleading copy in the cancellation path.
- No in-app cancellation for subscriptions sold in-app.
- Continued billing after cancellation via undisclosed "processing periods".
- Pause or downgrade options presented deceptively as the only exit.

## Best practices
- Apply the same-medium test: whatever channel enrolled the user must be able to cancel them at equal or lower effort.
- Time the cancellation path against the sign-up path; large asymmetry is a finding.
- Verify one or two clicks (or equivalent) suffice, with immediate confirmation and a clear effective date.
- Check that retention offers are honest, optional, and skippable without repeated modal pressure.
- Confirm billing stops at the promised point and written confirmation is sent.
- Require disclosure of any post-cancellation access or processing period at enrollment.
- Test the flow end-to-end with a real account, including verifying no further charges occur.
- Flag jurisdictions with click-to-cancel rules as compliance items for counsel.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Cancellation available in the same medium as sign-up.
- [ ] Effort to cancel ≤ effort to enroll.
- [ ] No mandatory calls, waits, or agent conversations.
- [ ] Retention copy honest and skippable.
- [ ] Immediate confirmation with clear effective date.
- [ ] Billing verifiably stops as promised.
- [ ] Post-cancel access terms disclosed at enrollment.
- [ ] End-to-end test performed on a real account.
