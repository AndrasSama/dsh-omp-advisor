# Update Schedule Optimization

Release cadence on serial platforms is both a visibility input and a habit-forming contract with readers.
More frequent releases generally earn more visibility, but an unsustainable pace burns the author out and breaks the schedule, which costs more than a slower consistent one; reviewers should optimize for the fastest pace the author can hold indefinitely.

## Watch for
- Announced schedules that don't match actual release history (claimed daily, actual twice a week)
- Release timing that misses the platform's peak-visibility window when new chapters are surfaced
- Zero chapter buffer: writing week to week, one emergency from a broken schedule
- Pace escalation during sprint events (e.g., Writathon) with no plan for the crash afterward
- Frequent schedule changes without announcement, eroding reader trust
- A cadence chosen for maximum visibility that the author describes as exhausting in their own notes
- Long gaps between arcs with no bridge content to hold readers

## Best practices
- Set cadence as the fastest sustainable pace: pick what can be held for six months, not two weeks
- Publish at consistent days and times; releases timed to site-peak hours earn stronger initial velocity
- Maintain a buffer of at least 2–4 finished chapters before launch and rebuild it after any disruption
- State the schedule in the blurb or a pinned post; announce any change before it happens
- Use sprint events deliberately, with a pre-written stockpile for the recovery period
- Front-load frequency at launch (daily for 1–2 weeks) when platforms reward new-release velocity, then settle
- Between arcs, keep cadence steady; bridge with interludes or side chapters rather than breaks

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Does the actual release history match the announced schedule?
- [ ] Are releases timed to the platform's peak-visibility window?
- [ ] Is there a buffer of 2+ finished chapters?
- [ ] Is the pace sustainable for six months at current life circumstances?
- [ ] Are schedule changes announced before they take effect?
- [ ] Is there a stockpile plan for sprint events and their aftermath?
- [ ] Is cadence steady across arc boundaries?
