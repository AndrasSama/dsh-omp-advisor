# Cross-Border Transfer Rules

GDPR Chapter V restricts transfers of personal data outside the EEA. Each transfer needs a mechanism — adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards (SCCs, BCRs), or an exceptional Article 49 derogation — and post-Schrems II, a documented assessment of the destination's laws. Review maps every transfer and tests the mechanism against current law.

## Watch for
- Personal data flowing to non-EEA countries with no Chapter V mechanism identified.
- Reliance on adequacy decisions that do not cover the specific territory or sector (check the current Commission list).
- SCCs using the wrong module (C2C, C2P, P2P, P2C under Decision 2021/914) or with clauses modified inconsistently with the Decision.
- No Transfer Impact Assessment for destinations with intrusive surveillance regimes, especially US transfers outside the Data Privacy Framework.
- US transfers relying on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework without verifying the importer's active certification.
- BCRs referenced but not approved, or not covering all group entities.
- Article 49 derogations used as a routine basis instead of exceptional cases.
- Onward-transfer restrictions missing from processor agreements.

## Best practices
- Map every transfer: exporter, importer, country, and mechanism.
- Apply the hierarchy: adequacy → appropriate safeguards (SCCs, BCRs) → Article 49 derogations (exceptional only).
- Use the correct SCC module and complete annexes; avoid modifications that contradict the Decision.
- Document a TIA for each material transfer: importer's legal environment, government-access risks, supplementary measures.
- For US importers, check DPF certification status and date; otherwise SCCs plus TIA.
- Verify BCR approval status and entity coverage.
- Apply supplementary measures where needed (encryption with exporter-held keys, pseudonymization, contractual commitments).
- Re-assess transfers on legal changes and re-paper SCCs when templates or relationships change.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] All transfers mapped with mechanism.
- [ ] Adequacy status current and applicable.
- [ ] Correct SCC module and annexes used.
- [ ] TIA documented for risky destinations.
- [ ] DPF certification verified if relied on.
- [ ] Derogations used exceptionally only.
- [ ] Onward-transfer restrictions in place.
