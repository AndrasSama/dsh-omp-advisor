# DMCA Takedown Drafting Review

The DMCA §512 safe-harbor process has strict element requirements for both takedown notices and counter-notices; a defective notice can fail to trigger the provider's obligations and can expose the sender to misrepresentation liability. The advisor reviews drafts for completeness and accuracy as process guidance — filing decisions and legal conclusions belong to counsel.

## Watch for
- Missing identification of the copyrighted work (or a representative list for bulk notices).
- Missing or vague location of the allegedly infringing material (URLs required, not descriptions).
- Absent good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
- Missing accuracy statement under penalty of perjury and a physical or electronic signature.
- No contact information for the complaining party.
- Counter-notices lacking consent to jurisdiction or the elements required for reinstatement.
- Notices targeting licensed, non-infringing, or plausible fair-use material (misrepresentation risk).
- Notices sent to hosts without a designated DMCA agent registered at the Copyright Office.

## Best practices
- Verify all §512(c)(3) elements are present before a notice goes out.
- Identify each work precisely; use a representative list only for works on a single site.
- Provide exact URLs and re-verify they still host the material at send time.
- Confirm ownership or authorization to act for the owner before drafting.
- Screen targets for licenses or plausible fair use to avoid misrepresentation claims.
- For counter-notices, check subscriber identity elements, consent to jurisdiction, and reinstatement address.
- Confirm the recipient host's designated agent via the Copyright Office directory.
- Route all notices and counter-notices through counsel review before filing.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Copyrighted work identified specifically
- [ ] Infringing material located by exact URL
- [ ] Good-faith statement present
- [ ] Perjury/accuracy statement and signature present
- [ ] Complainant contact information complete
- [ ] Ownership/authorization verified
- [ ] Target screened for license or fair use
- [ ] Designated agent confirmed and counsel looped in
