# Whistleblower Protection Ops

Sensitive sources risk their livelihoods and safety when they share information, and newsroom operational discipline is what protects them. This skill reviews the process around such sources: secure communication, anonymity hygiene, and awareness of protection frameworks. It is process guidance only; legal questions must go to qualified counsel.

## Watch for
- Contact with sensitive sources over unencrypted or employer-monitored channels.
- Metadata left in shared documents that could identify the source.
- Anonymity promises made before editorial consultation.
- Details in the story that narrow the source's identity to a handful of people.
- No plan for what happens if the source is exposed or faces retaliation.
- Reporters storing identifying material alongside story files.
- Ignoring the source's own exposure risk inside their workplace.
- Pressure tactics that push a source beyond their comfort or legal position.

## Best practices
- Move sensitive contact to vetted secure channels before substantive exchange.
- Strip metadata and sanitize documents before use or sharing.
- Agree anonymity terms with editors before publication decisions.
- Audit every published detail for mosaic-identification risk.
- Separate source-identifying material from reporting files; limit who holds it.
- Brief the source on likely consequences and let them set the pace.
- Involve editors and counsel early on high-risk material (process, not legal advice).
- Keep a contingency plan for exposure, subpoena, or retaliation scenarios.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Communications use vetted secure channels.
- [ ] Shared documents are metadata-stripped.
- [ ] Anonymity terms were agreed with editors in advance.
- [ ] A mosaic-identification audit was done on the draft.
- [ ] Identifying material is stored separately with limited access.
- [ ] The source was briefed on risks and consents at each step.
- [ ] Editors/counsel were engaged early on high-risk items.
- [ ] A contingency plan exists for exposure scenarios.
