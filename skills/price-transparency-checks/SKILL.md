# Price Transparency Checks

Consumers must see what they will actually pay before committing, and discount claims must be verifiable. This skill reviews pricing UX and claims against total-price display duties in EU consumer law, the Omnibus Directive price-history rule for reductions, and FTC deception principles around fees. Findings are review flags, not legal advice.

## Watch for
- Base price shown without unavoidable taxes, fees, or surcharges until late in checkout (drip pricing).
- Mandatory service, handling, or booking fees first revealed on the payment page.
- Optional extras preselected, inflating the effective price.
- Discount claims ("was €120, now €80") without a verifiable prior-price basis; Omnibus-style rules require disclosing the lowest price applied during a prior reference period (typically 30 days) for reductions.
- "From €X" teaser prices where the realistic configuration costs far more.
- Currency or unit ambiguity in cross-border displays.
- Personalized or dynamic pricing presented without disclosure where required.
- Installment plans that hide the total amount behind monthly figures.

## Best practices
- Walk the entire checkout as a first-time buyer and record the price shown at every step.
- Require the total price, including taxes and unavoidable fees, at the earliest point of display.
- For every reduction claim, ask for the disclosed prior-price basis and reference period.
- Verify optional extras are opt-in, never opt-out.
- Check that "from" prices are genuinely attainable, not theoretical minimums.
- Ensure installment displays show the total cost, not only the periodic amount.
- Compare advertised prices with amounts actually charged in test orders.
- Flag jurisdiction differences (EU Omnibus vs US all-in pricing regimes) as open items for counsel.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Total price incl. taxes/fees visible before checkout commitment.
- [ ] No mandatory fee first appears at the payment step.
- [ ] No preselected paid extras.
- [ ] Every reduction claim has a disclosed, verifiable prior-price basis.
- [ ] "From" prices reflect realistically attainable configurations.
- [ ] Currency and units unambiguous.
- [ ] Installment displays include total cost.
- [ ] Test-order charge matches the displayed total.
