# NIS2 Infrastructure Audit

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) extends cybersecurity obligations across essential and important entities in the EU, requiring risk-management measures under Article 21 and personal management accountability. The advisor reviews scope classification, control coverage, and governance evidence as readiness indicators — formal compliance determinations belong to the organization's legal and compliance functions.

## Watch for
- Organizations in NIS2 sectors (energy, transport, health, digital infrastructure, public administration) that have not classified themselves as essential or important.
- No documented risk analysis or information security policy covering Article 21 measure areas.
- Management not demonstrably approving or overseeing cybersecurity measures (accountability gap).
- Missing supply-chain security requirements for direct suppliers and service providers.
- No incident-handling process aligned with NIS2 reporting obligations.
- Business continuity and crisis-management plans absent or untested.
- No cyber-hygiene training or secure development lifecycle evidence.
- Reliance on a parent company or MSP without clarity on which entity carries the obligation.

## Best practices
- Confirm sector/annex classification and size thresholds to determine essential vs important status.
- Map existing controls to Article 21 areas: risk analysis, incident handling, continuity, supply chain, secure development, cryptography, access control, training.
- Evidence management approval: signed policies, board-level reporting, designated responsible persons.
- Extend security requirements into contracts with direct suppliers and managed service providers.
- Align incident reporting processes with NIS2 notification timelines.
- Test business continuity and crisis plans at least annually.
- Track member-state transposition and registration requirements where applicable.
- Record gaps with owners and remediation dates in a readiness register.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Entity classified essential/important with rationale
- [ ] Controls mapped to Article 21 measure areas
- [ ] Management accountability evidenced (approval, oversight)
- [ ] Supply-chain security requirements in supplier contracts
- [ ] Incident handling aligned with NIS2 reporting
- [ ] Business continuity plan tested
- [ ] Training and secure development lifecycle evidenced
- [ ] Gap register with owners and dates maintained
