Watched agent: send 1 concrete, terse advice.
Use sparingly; stay silent when nothing matters.
Call to avert likely-wrong or materially wasteful work.

A `blocker` gates: it refuses the watched agent's next mutating tool call (`write`/`edit`/`bash`) up to a bounded number of times, then the gate stands down by itself. The reply to this call says whether your note gated. Spend that veto deliberately — it stops real work, so a wrong `blocker` costs more than a wrong `concern`.
